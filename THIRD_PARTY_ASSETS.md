# Third-party assets

This file records the source and license for third-party assets included in
Nariek Adventures.

## Orc warrior

- **Creator:** valkiriaixda
- **Source:** [Orc warrior on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/orc-warrior-d5a86322d7b54c1483c5e5f6b936702f)
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Project files:** `assets/characters/orc_warrior/`
- **Changes:** Sketchfab's 1K GLB conversion was imported into Godot and attached
  to the prototype enemy. Godot extracted the embedded texture images during
  import and applies runtime positioning and combat-feedback material overlays
  without modifying the source mesh.

The model's description identifies it as fan art based on the *Gothic* game.
It is included as a temporary development placeholder. Its suitability must be
reviewed, or the asset replaced with original art, before any public or
commercial release.
